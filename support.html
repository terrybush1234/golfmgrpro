Section 1

Getting Started

Download the App

Search Golf League Manager Pro on the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. A one-time purchase of $9.99 unlocks unlimited leagues on your device.

First Launch

When you open the app for the first time you will be prompted to join an existing league or create a new one. The app requires an internet connection to sync data across all devices in your league.

Navigation

The app has five main tabs at the bottom of the screen:

  • Tee Sheet — view groups, tee times, and enter scores
  • Roster — sign up for game days and view game rules
  • Results — view blind draw teams and final standings
  • Admin — configure your league settings
  • Profile — manage your account
Section 2

Joining or Creating a League

Switching Between Leagues

Tap the league name banner at the top of the Tee Sheet to open the League Switcher. All leagues you have previously joined appear in the list. Tap Switch to jump between them instantly — no password required.

Joining an Existing League

  1. Tap the league name banner at the top of the Tee Sheet
  2. Tap Join / Create New League
  3. Enter the League ID provided by your commissioner
  4. Enter the League Password provided by your commissioner
  5. Tap Join — you are now connected to that league

Creating a New League

  1. Tap the league name banner at the top of the Tee Sheet
  2. Tap Join / Create New League
  3. Enter a unique League ID — this is the identifier other players will use to find your league (e.g. "friday_four" or "villa_app")
  4. You will be prompted to create a League Password — this is what other players enter when joining for the first time
  5. Tap Create — your league is created with a blank template ready for setup

Sharing Your League

Give your players the League ID and League Password. They enter these once when joining. After that they can switch to your league from their League Switcher without entering a password again.

Section 3

Admin Setup

Registering as Admin

Before you can edit any league settings you must claim admin access:

  1. Go to the Admin tab
  2. Tap Register Admin
  3. Enter your admin password
  4. Tap Confirm — admin is now locked to your device
Note
Only one device can hold admin access at a time. Other players can view settings but cannot change them.

League Banner

  • League Name — appears on all automated emails to the pro shop
  • Timezone — set to your local timezone so all automated triggers fire at the correct local time
  • Tap the green Save button to save changes

Pro Shop Email

Enter the pro shop's email address in the designated field. All automated communications — tee time requests, final player lists, and no-show notifications — are sent to this address automatically.

Season Dates

Set your Season Start and Season End dates. All automated emails and blind draws are suspended outside these dates so you never accidentally contact the pro shop during the off-season.

Tee Groups

Define the tee colors available at your course (e.g. Blue, White, Red, Gold). Once tee groups are configured, each player in the roster can be assigned a tee color. Tap ADD to add a color; tap the × on any chip to remove it. Removing a tee group clears that assignment from all players.

Game Settings

  • Game Mode
    • 1 Best Ball — 2-man teams. The tee draw always balances handicaps using snake distribution. At results time, if draw times match, partners are paired within tee sheet groups so you compete against the players you walk the course with. If draw times differ, partners are randomly paired across the field. Best net score per hole counts.
    • 2 Best Ball — tee sheet groups are the teams, always balanced by snake distribution. Works with twosomes, threesomes, and foursomes. Two best net scores per group per hole count.
    • Quota — individual stableford-style points vs a personal target based on handicap.
  • All Net Birdies or Better Count — optional toggle for Best Ball modes. When enabled, if more players than the normal threshold make net birdie or better on a hole, all of their scores count.
  • Course Length — 9 or 18 holes
  • Bet Amount — entry fee per player displayed on results
  • Skins Fee — skins entry fee per player
  • Handicap Allowance % — 0% to 100% to adjust how much handicap is applied to net scores
  • Gross Birdie Override (skins) — when enabled, a gross birdie or better wins the skin outright. A net birdie cannot beat a gross birdie.

Passwords

  • League Password — what players enter when joining for the first time
  • Admin Password — only for the commissioner to claim admin access. Keep this private.

Group Message

Set a recurring automated message sent to all league members with an email on file. Enter the message text, select the day and time to send it. Useful for weekly sign-up reminders.

Section 4

Day Manager

The Day Manager is the heart of the app. Tap any day button (Sun through Sat) to configure that game day. Days highlighted in green are active game days.

Activating a Day

Inside the day settings modal tap Activate This Day to make it a play day. Deactivate it at any time to suppress all emails and draws for that day.

Tee Times

Enter the tee times for that day. The number of tee times × 4 sets the player cap automatically. For example, 3 tee times = maximum 12 players. These times are also included in the final player list email sent to the pro shop.

Lockout Time

The time when sign-ups close on game day. At this time:

  • The roster is frozen — no new sign-ups or cancellations
  • The blind tee draw runs automatically
  • The final player list is emailed to the pro shop grouped by tee time

Results Draw Time

The time when the Results tab unlocks and teams are paired. The draw runs automatically — no one needs to have the app open. If set to the same time as Lockout, results teams are drawn from within tee sheet groups so partners play together. If set to a different time, partners are randomly paired across the field.

Pro Shop Advance Request

Configure an automated tee time request sent to the pro shop before game day. Set the trigger day and time (typically 2–3 days before game day). The email counts current sign-ups, rounds up to the nearest foursome, and requests that many tee times. The player cap is updated automatically once sent.

Section 5

Course Setup

Entering Hole Data

The course grid shows all 18 holes (or 9 if configured). For each hole enter:

  • Par — the par for that hole
  • Index — the handicap stroke index (1 = hardest, 18 = easiest)

Fields highlighted in blue need to be filled in. Once entered they turn white.

Course Library

  1. Enter par and index for each hole
  2. Tap Save As... and give the course a name
  3. The course is saved to your library and can be loaded at any time from the dropdown
  4. Tap Add New to start a fresh blank course
Section 6

Roster & Sign-Up

Claiming Your Player Profile

Before you can sign up for any game day you must claim your player profile. This is a one-time setup.

  1. Go to the Roster tab
  2. Find your name and tap it
  3. Tap Claim — your profile is now locked to this device
Tip
Your claim persists across weeks and app updates. If you get a new phone, the commissioner can release your claim so you can re-claim on your new device.

Signup Status

Each signup cycles through three states:

  • ⭕ OUT — not playing
  • ✅ GAME — playing, entering the bet
  • 💎 SKINS — playing and entering skins

Player Cap

Once a game day reaches its cap it shows FULL and no additional players can sign up. The commissioner can increase the cap in Day Manager settings.

Handicap Management

Player handicaps are managed in the Admin tab. Handicap fields auto-save 1.5 seconds after you stop typing.

Section 7

Tee Sheet & Blind Draw

Before Lockout Time

The tee sheet shows a countdown to the draw time. Players can see who is signed up but groups have not been assigned yet.

At Lockout Time

The blind draw runs automatically. Players are distributed across tee groups using a snake distribution by handicap — lowest handicap paired with highest, ensuring every group has a balanced mix of skill levels. Groups are assigned to tee times and the tee sheet updates immediately. The pro shop receives the final player list by email.

Viewing and Claiming a Scorecard

  • View Scorecard — opens the scorecard in read-only mode. Available to all players at any time after the draw.
  • Claim & Score — claims the scorecard for your device and opens it for score entry. Once claimed, the button changes to Edit Scorecard.

Moving a Player to a Different Tee Time

  1. Tap the tee time shown next to the player's name
  2. A list of all available tee times appears
  3. Select the new tee time
  4. The player is immediately reassigned and the tee sheet updates
1 Best Ball Note
If Lockout Time and Results Draw Time are the same, moving a player also moves their draw partner to the same tee slot and the results draw is rebuilt. If the two draw times differ, only the selected player moves.

Forcing a Tee Draw

Commissioners can force the tee draw at any time by tapping 🎲 Force Tee Draw in the admin controls. This overwrites any existing tee sheet for the selected day. Requires at least 2 signed-up players.

Section 8

Scorecard & Live Scoring

Claiming a Scorecard

Each group has one scorecard. One player per group claims it:

  1. Tap Claim & Score on your group's tee time banner
  2. The scorecard opens — only your device can now enter scores for this group

Other players and anyone watching will see scores update live as they are entered.

Entering Scores

  • Enter the gross score for each player on each hole
  • The app automatically calculates net scores based on handicap and course index
  • Running totals update in real time
  • Maximum score per hole is enforced based on handicap plus two strokes

DNF (Did Not Finish)

If a player cannot complete the round tap the DNF toggle next to their name. They are excluded from the results draw.

Saving Scores

  • Save Scores — saves current scores and closes the scorecard. The app refreshes after 2 seconds.
  • Close & Exit — saves all scores and closes the scorecard.

Releasing a Locked Scorecard

If a player's phone dies mid-round: go to the Admin tab → tap 🔓 Reset Locks. Another player in the group can then claim the scorecard and continue entering scores.

Section 9

Results & Leaderboard

Results Tab Lock

The Results tab is locked until the results draw time set in Day Manager. Before that time it shows a countdown — this prevents players from seeing team pairings before the round.

The Results Draw

At the results draw time, teams are paired automatically — no one needs to have the app open. In 1 Best Ball, if draw time matches lockout time, partners are paired within tee sheet groups; otherwise randomly across the field. In 2 Best Ball, tee sheet groups become teams automatically. In Quota mode each player competes individually.

Scoring Display

All Best Ball team scores display as +/− relative to par. E = even par, −2 = two under, +1 = one over.

Color Coding

  • Blue — the team's round is still in progress
  • Green — all players in the team have completed their round

Calculating Results

  1. Go to the Results tab
  2. Tap Calculate
  3. Final standings appear showing team net scores, skins winners, and payouts

Skins

Skins are calculated hole by hole from all players who paid the skins fee. The lowest net score on a hole wins outright. If two or more players tie, the skin carries over to the next hole. When Gross Birdie Override is enabled, a gross birdie or better wins the skin automatically.

Clearing Results After a Score Correction

  1. Fix the score on the scorecard
  2. Go to Admin → tap 🧹 Clear Result Teams and Redraw (Keep Scores)
  3. The draw clears and rebuilds automatically — scores are preserved
Section 10

Pro Shop Communications

All pro shop emails are sent automatically. No action is required from the commissioner on game day.

A. Advance Tee Time Request
Sent on the day and time configured in Day Manager. Counts signed-up players, rounds up to the nearest foursome, requests that many tee times, and sets the player cap. Subject: Tee Time Request — [Date]
B. Final Player List
Sent automatically at lockout time. Lists all players grouped by tee time. Subject: FINAL PLAYER LIST: [League Name] — [Date]
C. No Players Notification
Sent automatically at lockout time if no players have signed up. Notifies the pro shop that no tee times are needed. Subject: NO PLAYERS TODAY: [League Name] — [Date]
D. No Tee Times Needed (Advance)
If the advance request fires and there are no sign-ups yet, the pro shop receives an advance notice that no tee times will be needed.
Deactivating a Day
Go to Day Manager → tap the day → tap Deactivate This Day. All emails for that day are automatically suppressed.
Section 11

Weekly Reset

Every Sunday at 3am the app automatically resets all leagues for the new week.

What is cleared

  • All sign-ups
  • All tee assignments
  • All scores and score maps
  • All results draws

What is rebuilt

  • Player caps — automatically set from the number of tee times configured in Day Manager for each active game day

What is kept

  • All members and their handicaps
  • All league settings
  • Course setup
  • Season dates
  • Tee group assignments
  • Player device claims
Lookback
The current week's scores remain available until the following Sunday reset. After reset those scores are permanently removed.
Section 12

Commissioner Tools

Available in the Admin tab to registered admins only.

🔓 Reset Locks
Releases all scorecard claims for the current day. Use this if a player's phone fails mid-round so another player in the group can take over scoring.
🧹 Clear Result Teams and Redraw (Keep Scores)
Clears the results team pairings and flags for the current day only. All entered scores are preserved. Use this after correcting a score error — you do not need to re-enter scores.
🎲 Force Tee Draw
Runs the blind tee sheet draw immediately using handicap-based snake distribution, overwriting any existing tee sheet for the selected day. Requires at least 2 players signed up.
🏆 Force Results Draw
Runs the results team pairing immediately without waiting for the scheduled draw time. Existing scores are not affected. Requires at least 2 players.
🔄 Reset Week (Keep Roster)
Clears all week data — sign-ups, tee assignments, scores, and results draws — and reloads the app. The member roster and all league settings are fully preserved. Use only as a last resort.
📵 Unclaim Player
Releases a player's profile claim so they can re-claim on a new device. Only the commissioner can unclaim a player.
Delete League & Admin Account
Permanently deletes all league data and releases admin access. This cannot be undone. Use with extreme caution.
Section 13

Troubleshooting

Scores not updating after closing scorecard
After pressing Save Scores or Close & Exit the app automatically refreshes after 2 seconds. You will see "🔄 Refreshing scores..." before the update. If scores still appear outdated, tap the Refresh button on the tee sheet.
Date picker showing wrong week
The date picker is constrained to the current golf week (Sunday through Saturday). If you have just come back from a previous week the calendar auto-advances to the current week on the next load.
Roster shows FULL but no one is signed up
No cap has been set for that day. Go to Day Manager, open the settings for that day, confirm the tee times are entered, and tap Save. The cap will be set immediately.
Can't see my sign-up days on the Roster tab
You need to claim your player profile first. Tap your name on the Roster tab and tap Claim. Once claimed your day slots will be visible.
Tapped another player's name by mistake
If that player has already claimed their profile you will see a message that it is taken and no changes will be made. If they have not yet claimed, contact your commissioner.
Player got a new phone and can't claim their profile
The player's old device still holds the claim. Go to Admin → find the player in the roster → tap Unclaim. The player can then open the app on their new device, tap their name, and tap Claim.
Pro shop did not receive the email
Check that the pro shop email address is correctly entered in Admin settings, the game day is activated in Day Manager, and the lockout time has passed. Also check the pro shop's spam or junk folder.
League settings changed by another player
Only the registered admin can change settings. Go to Admin → Register Admin to reclaim access.
Scorecard locked and player's phone is unavailable
Go to Admin → 🔓 Reset Locks to release all scorecard claims so another player can take over.
App shows old league after switching
Tap the league name banner and tap Switch on the correct league. The app will reload with the selected league's current data.
Results tab shows "Drawing..." but teams never appear
The draw runs automatically at the results draw time. If it does not appear within a minute tap the Results tab again to refresh. If the issue persists go to Admin → 🏆 Force Results Draw.
Need to sign back in after update
Anonymous sessions are cleared every 30 days. If you are prompted to rejoin simply re-enter your League ID and password — all your data and scores are preserved in the database.
